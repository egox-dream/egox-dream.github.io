EgoX embodiment visualizations
=============================

These posed visualization models derive from the approved EgoX Blender figures.
The web copies are static glTF 2.0 geometry with Draco compression. Body axes
are aligned to the EgoX convention, models retain meter units, and each asset is
centered and grounded for an independent orbit viewer. Left end-effectors are
purple; right end-effectors are orange. The simulation G1 retains its blue body.
CAD components may be simplified for browser rendering. Model dimensions,
display poses, inferred adapters, and the schematic human are illustrations,
not replacement robot descriptions or mechanical calibration data.

Sources and retained notices
----------------------------

Human-Dex5: custom schematic EgoX human visualization, with the approved
enlarged hand display and no camera-marker ball.

G1-Dex5 and G1-Sim-Dex3: local EgoX G1 robot and end-effector mesh bundles.
The G1 source documentation attributes the body to Unitree's G1 description:
https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_ros/blob/master/robots/g1_description/g1_29dof_with_hand_rev_1_0.urdf
Real G1 uses the local Inspire F1 hands; simulated G1 uses the three-finger hand.
No asset-specific license was found in the local G1/Inspire F1 bundles.

xArm7-Dex4: local EgoX xArm7 description and LEAP hand geometry. The LEAP
notice credits Ananye Agarwal (2023) and documents later dex_urdf modifications.
Retained notice: licenses/LEAP-MIT.txt
No asset-specific license was found in the local xArm7 body mesh bundle.

LocoMan-Dex2: local EgoX Go1-based LocoMan mesh bundle; approved right-reaching,
left-folded illustration. No asset-specific license was found in that bundle.

abe7f5161eeaa150e6eaffdf443af5df7f23f356:
https://github.com/OpenGalaxea/GalaxeaManipSim/tree/abe7f5161eeaa150e6eaffdf443af5df7f23f356/galaxea_sim/assets/r1_lite/
Retained repository Apache-2.0 notice: licenses/R1Lite-Apache-2.0.txt

ccfc6fd8430a17ba3dacef9a1e2faf64ff3b0aee:
https://github.com/unitreerobotics/unitree_ros/tree/ccfc6fd8430a17ba3dacef9a1e2faf64ff3b0aee/robots/z1_description

The scoop is user-supplied scoop_1.STEP CAD. Its U bracket and fasteners are
visual approximations based on the supplied robot photos. These custom parts
are not assigned the manufacturer repository's license. The web model uses the
approved corrected terminal mounting and START_MANIPULATION reset pose.

No license is invented for a locally unlabelled asset, and neighboring code's
license is not automatically assigned to a mesh. Keep these original notices
and provenance with copies of the distributed visualizations.
